Pedigree & History

Old-Money Address.
New-Age Home.

Bani Park has been one of Jaipur’s respected residential neighbourhoods for generations — tree-lined margs, gracious homes and a quiet that feels increasingly rare this close to the heart of the city.

It sits beside the historic Pink City, under the presence of Nahargarh Fort and within easy reach of MI Road, the Collectorate and Jaipur Railway Station.

"You can’t manufacture heritage. You can only build thoughtfully within it. Pink Oaks brings contemporary luxury to a neighbourhood that already has pedigree — so while your home is new, your address comes with history."
